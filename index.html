06 — FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

Will this actually work for real estate?

It's what the system was built for. Vendors decide who to trust before they ever book an appraisal — usually from what they see of you online. We build the funnel that wins that decision: authentic creative, a site that positions you as the obvious choice, and a chatbot that captures the enquiry the moment it happens. Ecommerce runs on the same logic with a shorter loop.

What if I already have an agency?

Keep them if they're converting. If you're paying retainers for polished creative that doesn't book work, that's exactly the problem we exist to fix. Book the discovery call, show us your numbers, and we'll tell you honestly whether we'd do better. If we wouldn't, we'll say so.

What's the minimum commitment?

Creative partnerships are month to month. Account management carries a 3-month minimum and a minimum ad spend, because paid campaigns need at least one full optimisation cycle to prove out. Website and chatbot builds are one-off builds with an ongoing management fee you can end any time.

How fast do I see results?

The system is live on day 21. Leads typically start moving within the first fortnight of deployment; meaningful, decision-grade data lands inside the first optimisation cycle — around 90 days. Anyone promising overnight results is selling you hope, not a system.

What do you need from me during the build?

Three things: the 30-minute discovery call, a sign-off on strategy at day 3, and a walkthrough with your decision-makers at day 18. Plus raw material — real photos, real people, real wins. Authenticity converts; we can't fabricate it and won't try.

Is the intro pricing really locked in?

Yes. The rate you sign at is the rate you keep for the life of your engagement, in writing. Prices rise as the roster fills — that only affects new clients, never you.

What ad spend do I need?

For creative-only partnerships, whatever you're spending now. For managed campaigns we set a minimum spend at quotation — enough for the algorithm to learn and the data to mean something. We'd rather turn you away than take a budget that can't win.

Do you handle the ads or just the creative?

Your choice. The creative partnership feeds your existing media buyer. Add account management and we run the lot — setup, targeting, budget optimisation, reporting. Most clients start with creative and hand over the accounts once they've seen it convert.

What happens after deployment?

Day 21 is the start line, not the finish. From there it's iteration: refreshing creative before it fatigues, feeding winners, cutting losers, and reporting in numbers you can read in 30 seconds. The system compounds — that's the point of owning one.
